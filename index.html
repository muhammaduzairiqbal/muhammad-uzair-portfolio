FINAL YEAR PROJECT · OCT 2026 — PRESENT

Quranify

AI Quran Tutor

Quranify helps learners practise Quran recitation with AI feedback. A learner recites an ayah, the speech model transcribes and compares it against the authentic text, and the app highlights where the recitation differs — covering the complete Quran: all 114 Surahs and 6,236 Ayahs.

  • Speech recognition pipeline — PyTorch model tuned to a 0.50% character error rate and 9.2% word error rate on recitation.
  • Real-time feedback — WebSocket streaming cut response latency from 436 ms to 255 ms.
  • Tested & shipped properly — 55/55 automated tests passing, Dockerised services, FastAPI backend with PostgreSQL & SQLite.
  • Team project — built together with my FYP partner Mudasir at Abasyn University.
